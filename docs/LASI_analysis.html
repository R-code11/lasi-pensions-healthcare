2.1 Outpatient

Note: The sample consists of individuals aged 60-100 years at the time of the survey. Old Age Pension, Widow Pension, and Annapurna Pension are separate indicators for receipt of the respective pension benefits. Any Pension is an indicator equal to one if the respondent receives at least one of the Old Age, Widow, or Annapurna pensions. Outpatient healthcare utilization is an indicator equal to one if the respondent consulted a healthcare provider (doctors, AYUSH practitioners, dentists, nurses, and traditional healers) during the last 12 months. Additional Controls include age, gender, educational attainment, marital status (1 = married, 0 = widowed; other categories are excluded), rural or urban residence, religion, caste category, and monthly per capita consumption expenditure (MPCE) quintile. Standard errors clustered at PSU level are in parentheses.
* p<0.10, ** p<0.05, *** p<0.01
Source: Authors' calculations from LASI

4 Tobit and Negative Binomial (Number of OPD Visits)

Note: The sample consists of individuals aged 60-100 years at the time of the survey. Old Age Pension, Widow Pension, and Annapurna Pension are separate indicators for receipt of the respective pension benefits. Any Pension is an indicator equal to one if the respondent receives at least one of the Old Age, Widow, or Annapurna pensions. Number of OPD visits is the number of outpatient visits to a healthcare provider during the last 12 months. The count includes respondents with zero visits. Odd-numbered columns report Tobit estimates left-censored at zero; even-numbered columns report negative binomial (NB) estimates with a log link. State fixed effects enter as state dummies. Coefficients are not marginal effects, so the sign and significance are the main focus. Additional Controls include age, gender, educational attainment, marital status (1 = married, 0 = widowed; other categories are excluded), rural or urban residence, religion, caste category, and monthly per capita consumption expenditure (MPCE) quintile. Standard errors clustered at PSU level are in parentheses.
* p<0.10, ** p<0.05, *** p<0.01
Source: Authors' calculations from LASI

Note: The sample consists of individuals aged 60-100 years at the time of the survey. Any Pension is an indicator equal to one if the respondent receives at least one of the Old Age, Widow, or Annapurna pensions. 6 Probit (Outpatient)

Note: The sample consists of individuals aged 60-100 years at the time of the survey. Old Age Pension, Widow Pension, and Annapurna Pension are separate indicators for receipt of the respective pension benefits. Any Pension is an indicator equal to one if the respondent receives at least one of the Old Age, Widow, or Annapurna pensions. Outpatient healthcare utilization is an indicator equal to one if the respondent consulted a healthcare provider (doctors, AYUSH practitioners, dentists, nurses, and traditional healers) during the last 12 months. Coefficients are probit estimates, and each comes from a separate regression. AME is the average marginal effect and MEM is the marginal effect at the means, both computed from equivalent models with state fixed effects entered as dummies. Additional Controls include age, gender, educational attainment, marital status (1 = married, 0 = widowed; other categories are excluded), rural or urban residence, religion, caste category, and monthly per capita consumption expenditure (MPCE) quintile. Standard errors clustered at PSU level are in parentheses.
* p<0.10, ** p<0.05, *** p<0.01
Source: Authors' calculations from LASI
